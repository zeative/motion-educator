# Where these maps come from

- `earth-day.jpg`: NASA Blue Marble (NASA Earth Observatory), a cloud-free colour map of the Earth.
- `earth-night.jpg`: NASA Earth at Night ("Black Marble", NASA Earth Observatory / NOAA NGDC), scaled to 2048 x 1024.
- `earth-water.jpg`: a land and water mask, scaled to 1024 x 512 and saved as greyscale.

NASA's imagery is not copyrighted. These copies were taken from the example images of the
[three-globe](https://github.com/vasturiano/three-globe) package (MIT licence), version 2.45.6.
