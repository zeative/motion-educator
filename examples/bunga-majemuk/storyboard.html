PromiseDengan bunga majemuk, bunganya ikut berbunga, jadi uang tumbuh melengkung ke atas, bukan lurus; dan aturan 72 (72 dibagi bunga per tahun) kira-kira memberi tahu berapa tahun uang jadi dua kali lipat.

f1Simple interest (bunga diambil tiap tahun): 10% of Rp10 juta = Rp1 juta a year, every year, because the pokok never changes. After 30 years: 10 + 30 x 1 = Rp40 juta in all (the Rp10 juta pokok plus Rp30 juta taken out). Drawn as the line 10 + t.Definition of simple interest (interest on the principal only), e.g. Investor.gov (US SEC), Compound Interest Calculator and glossary; arithmetic done here.
f2Compounded once a year (bunga dibiarkan): the balance after t years is 10 x 1,1^t juta. Year 1: 11. Year 2's interest: 0,1 x 11 = 1,1 juta = 1 (from the pokok) + 0,1 (from last year's interest: 'bunga dari bunga'). Year 1 to 5 interest: 1; 1,1; 1,21; 1,331; 1,4641. Year 5: 16,11 vs 15 simple (gap 1,1). Year 30: 10 x 1,1^30 = 174,494 ≈ Rp174,5 juta (said 'hampir seratus tujuh puluh lima juta'). 174,5 / 40 = 4,36 ≈ 4,4×.Compound interest formula A = P(1 + r)^n, e.g. Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, Open Vault, 'How Does Compound Interest Work?' (2018); arithmetic done here (Python).
f3Rule of 72: years to double ≈ 72 / yearly rate in percent. At 10%: 72 / 10 = 7,2 years (said 'kira-kira tujuh tahun'). The exact doubling time at 10% compounded yearly is ln 2 / ln 1,1 = 7,27 years, so the ladder's corners are drawn at 7,27; 14,55; 21,82; 29,09 years for Rp20, 40, 80 and 160 juta, and the marks under the axis are rounded (≈7, ≈15, ≈22, ≈29). The rule is an estimate, closest for rates around 6 to 10%.Investor.gov (US SEC), glossary: 'Rule of 72'; Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, Open Vault (2018); exact time computed here.
f410% a year is an illustration chosen to make the arithmetic easy, not the return of any product; the film says so on screen. It ignores tax, fees, inflation and changing rates, and compounds once a year. The film teaches the mechanism and gives no financial advice.The film's own framing; simplifications listed here.
f5In the pictures one coin stands for Rp1 juta and a slice a tenth of a coin's thickness for Rp100 ribu (10% of one coin). Ten slices make one coin (year 1's Rp1 juta), eleven make 1,1 coins (year 2's Rp1,1 juta). The columns in s14 are 3,9 px per Rp1 juta: 39 px at year 0, 680,5 px at year 30.Arithmetic from f1 and f2.
