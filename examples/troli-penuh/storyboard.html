Kenapa troli penuh susah didorong?Kenapa troli belanja yang penuh lebih susah didorong, padahal beratnya nggak kita angkat?

PromiseYang bikin troli penuh susah didorong itu massanya, bukan beratnya: dorongan yang sama ke massa tiga kali lipat cuma menghasilkan sepertiga percepatan, dan dia juga tiga kali lebih susah dihentikan.
AudienceOrang Indonesia yang belanja ke supermarket dan nonton video pendek. Mereka tahu troli penuh terasa berat, tapi mengira itu soal berat yang harus diangkat atau ditahan, bukan soal massa.

f1A real supermarket trolley: the Wanzl ELT7/130, with a 131-litre basket, weighs 21 kg (load capacity 131 kg). The film calls the empty trolley 'sekitar dua puluh satu kilo' and writes '21 kg' and the model on screen.Wanzl, Shopping trolleys catalogue (1193_Shopping-trolleys_EN.pdf, wanzl.com), 'Order section for ELT series': ELT7/130, basket volume 131 l, load capacity 131 kg, weight 21.0 kg.
f2On a level floor you do not lift the trolley: its weight is carried by the wheels and held up by the floor (the floor's upward push balances the weight). Simplification: the film draws two equal arrow pairs; there are four wheels.NASA Glenn Research Center, Beginner's Guide, 'Newton's Laws of Motion' and 'Weight' (grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12): a body at rest has balanced forces.
f3Inertia (Newton's first law): a body at rest stays at rest, and a body in motion keeps moving at the same speed, unless an outside force acts on it.NASA Glenn Research Center, 'Newton's First Law of Motion' (grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/newton1g.html).
f4Newton's second law: F = m × a, so a = F / m. With the same push F, three times the mass gives a third of the acceleration. Starting from rest for the same time t, the distance is a t² / 2, so the full trolley covers a third as far (the film's lanes: 640 px and 213 px).NASA Glenn Research Center, 'Newton's Second Law of Motion' (grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/BGP/newton2.html); 'Motion equations' (grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/motion.html) for distance = a t² / 2.
f5The shopping is an example: 42 kg (a big week's shop: drinks, rice, detergent). 21 + 42 = 63 kg, and 63 / 21 = 3: three times the empty trolley's mass. On screen: '21 + 42 = 63 kg' and '3×'.Arithmetic from f1; the 42 kg is chosen for the example and is said as one ('Tambah belanjaan empat puluh dua kilo').
f6Stopping: with the same braking force F from the same speed v, the deceleration is F / m and the stopping distance is v² / (2a) = m v² / (2F), proportional to the mass. Three times the mass: three times the distance (the film's marks: 190 px and 570 px). Rolling friction is ignored in both lanes.NASA Glenn Research Center, 'Newton's Second Law of Motion' and 'Motion equations' (v² = 2 a d), as f4; the arithmetic is shown in storyboard.build.py.
